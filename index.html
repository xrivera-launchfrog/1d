Our Services

STRATEGIC ADVISORY SERVICES

We develop bespoke strategies for technology companies, investment firms and corporations on how to best engage the Federal market. This includes crafting go-to-market strategies, conducting due diligence, assessing the market demand, and enabling the transition from R&D contracts to a Program of Record.

DATA-DRIVEN TECHNOLOGY SCOUTING

We offer technology and supply chain solutions enabled through COMPASS, our machine learning tool that uses quantitative assessments of global technology trends to enable competitor tech analysis, tech roadmap forecasting, and 2nd/3rd order supply chain identification.

VENTURE STUDIO

We transform promising new technology concepts into viable and scalable dual-purpose ventures by rigorously validating new business concepts, then building full-stack teams of best in class experts and coaching them from a minimum viable product to an upscaled venture, seeded with investment capital.

About Us

We have assembled a team of military, policy, and corporate leaders that understand operational requirements, the technology development process, and the business practices to meet the TDA vision and to make the national security market accessible to dual-use technologies.
