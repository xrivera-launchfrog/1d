We Develop Dual-Use Technology Strategies

One Defense enables technology entrepreneurs and investments in the Aerospace, Defense & Government Services sector. We partner with venture capital, executives-in-residence and select businesses that seek to provide a qualitative/quantitative edge to American national security.

One Defense has assisted dozens of domestic & international venture-backed technology companies seeking to support the United States Department of Defense, Department of State and Intelligence Community. Our work for these clients range from advising on strategic acquisitions to acting as operating partners for technology firms on behalf of venture and private equity stakeholders.
